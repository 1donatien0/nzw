The app

Nyimbo za Wokovu is a free Android app with 335 Christian songs in Swahili. Every verse is included in the app: it works without an Internet connection. These songs teach salvation, faith, hope and new life in Christ.

Features

  • 335 songsEvery verse, with author and source — 1,265 stanzas
  • SearchBy title or by song number
  • Song of the dayDaily notification; tap to open the song
  • 21 groupsSongs arranged by theme
  • FavouritesKept even after updates
  • OfflineNo ads, no data collected

Privacy

The app collects no personal information and sends nothing to third-party servers. Your favourites, history and display settings stay on your device. Details in the privacy policy.
